In the Hold of Destiny

By K. T. Tran

This is not a small story. This is a multi-generational story that recounts the history of Vietnam from its colonial period to modern times. It follows Hòa, an ordinary Vietnamese villager, as she experiences childhood in her village, witnesses the persecution of her family, and struggles through the downfall of the southern Vietnamese government. Through this recounting, this story captures not only her life but also serves as a documentation of vanishing tradition and culture.

On the Flooded Riverbanks

In the Hold of Destiny Book One

As the first book in this two-part series, On the Flooded Riverbanks focuses on Hòa's early life. What begins as peaceful rural living quickly vanishes as Hòa is thrust into the reality of politics during the First Indochina War when the North Vietnamese government begins its campaign to eliminate landowners.

About the Author

K.T. Tran is a Vietnamese immigrant and self-taught artist. What had started as a promise to her late mother became a decade-long project to record and assemble the story of her parents and Vietnam's history through their lens. This is her first foray into the world of book writing.

Reviews

"This set of books is a beautifully woven tale comprised of both memoir and historical text, rich with the culture, folklore, and sentiments of people at the time. It gives history from the perspective that I consider most important—not from those with names listed in the history books, but through the eyes of the unnamed people whose lives were most impacted."

"We follow the main character, Hòa, through the decades—from simple rural life to the ongoing repercussions of war. We see the resilience of everyday people just trying to live their lives against the constant backdrop of political turmoil, yet also we experience the mundaneness of death when tragedy is around every corner. The cumulative experiences of Hòa's life are extraordinary, and if I did not know for a fact that these narratives are accurate to her first-person account, I would never believe this life story to be true. At its root, this is a story of sacrifice—the kind that is intrinsic to your soul, that you choose to make every day, every year, fueled by the glimmer of hope that your loved ones will have a better tomorrow."

— Dr. Nancy Nguyen, Hòa's Granddaughter
